## 🎮 What You Can Do

### As STUDENT:

#### Upload Notes:
1. Fill title, subject, description
2. Choose PDF file
3. Click "Upload Note"
4. Status: Pending

#### Edit Your Notes:
1. Go to "My Uploaded Notes"
2. Click "Edit" button
3. Change title/subject/description
4. Click "Save Changes"
5. Status changes to Pending again

#### Delete Your Notes:
1. Go to "My Uploaded Notes"
2. Click "Delete" button
3. Confirm deletion
4. Note removed permanently

#### Search Notes:
1. Use search form
2. Enter keywords or subject
3. View all approved notes
4. Click to view PDF
5. Download and rate

### As ADMIN:

#### Approve/Reject:
1. View all notes in table
2. Click green ✓ to approve
3. Click orange ✗ to reject

#### Delete Any Note:
1. Click red trash icon
2. Confirm deletion

#### Upload Notes:
- Admin can also upload notes
- Same as student process
